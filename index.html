Case Study

Building an original content franchise

How one simple story idea became a repeatable, high-performing series, and how I kept evolving it based on audience response.

The Assassin Who Can't Resist Cute Animals

The concept: an assassin on a mission to eliminate bad guys and save the world keeps getting distracted by adorable creatures. Every time, he abandons the mission, takes the animal home and builds it a cozy new habitat.

Each episode is cut from my own raw gameplay and roleplay recordings and narrated with my own voiceover. A tense, mission-style opening sets expectations, then a wholesome twist flips them, a pattern interrupt that drives watch time and shares.

743K+combined organic views across 3 featured episodes
1

Concept & hook

Designed a simple, instantly understood premise with a built-in twist: tension in the first seconds, wholesome payoff at the end.

2

Proved the formula

Three episodes released within days of each other (a dragonfly, a spider and a red panda) each passed 100K views, with the spider episode reaching 453K. That confirmed the twist as a repeatable format, not a one-off.

3

Iterated on the format

Varied each setup (sniper scope, jungle journey, knife mission) so the series stayed fresh while keeping its signature twist and branded title card.

4

Evolved into edutainment

Newer episodes feature real-world animals, followed by a mini documentary segment where I narrate real fun facts while the animal explores its new home.

5

Expanded to long-form

Launched The Masked Wanderer, a long-form horror roleplay series on YouTube, extending the assassin character into full-length storytelling with a cinematic trailer intro.
